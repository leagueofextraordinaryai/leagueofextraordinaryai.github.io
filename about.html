Building AI that elevates human judgment

Our mission is to build AI systems that accurately simulate complex individual and group dynamics and transform those simulations into powerful training tools. By combining advanced modeling, behavioral science, and interactive learning, we help people understand human behavior, improve decision-making, and navigate complex social environments. Our goal is to elevate human judgment, collaboration, and performance across education, leadership, healthcare, security, and enterprise.
