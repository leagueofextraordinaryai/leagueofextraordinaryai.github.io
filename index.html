Core Research

Simulating human decision
at every scale

Our research combine behavioral science, multi-agent AI, and learning science to create training environments that mirror the complexity of real human situations.

Human Behavior Modeling
Our AI models the cognitive, social, and emotional dimensions of individual and group behavior using established behavioral science research. Simulated agents think, react, and interact with the nuance of real people, creating training scenarios that feel credible and transfer more effectively to real-world situations.
Autonomous Agent Architecture
Each simulated individual operates with private beliefs, goals, emotional states, and decision-making heuristics. Agents adapt to unfolding events, respond to one another, and form realistic interaction patterns that make training environments feel socially rich and human.
Multi-Agent Group Dynamics
Simulate teams, crowds, organizations, and communities with agents that influence one another in realistic ways. Our multi-agent engine reveals emergent behavior, social pressure, and collective decision-making patterns that simple models often miss.
Personalized Adaptive Training
Simulations respond dynamically to trainee actions instead of following a static script. They escalate difficulty, surface edge cases, and personalize feedback in real time to accelerate learning and strengthen retention.
Applications

Transforming decision-making and training across domains

Education & Training
Help learners and educators rehearse high-stakes scenarios inside adaptive, AI-driven environments. Build stronger reasoning, communication, and decision-making through consequence-rich practice.
Defense & Security
Prepare teams for complex human terrain with realistic simulations of adversaries, civilians, and allies. Develop better judgment, coordination, and cultural awareness under pressure.
Leadership & Management
Train leaders in realistic organizational scenarios shaped by conflict, ambiguity, and team dynamics. Practice communication, influence, and decision-making before stakes become real.
Healthcare & Clinical Training
Support clinicians and care teams with simulated patient interactions and decision-rich training. Improve communication, empathy, and clinical judgment across diverse care situations.
Research & Insights
Test interventions and predict responses with AI-simulated populations before real-world rollout. Explore behavioral patterns at scale without the cost and delay of live experiments.
How It Works

From scenario design to real-world impact

1
Define the Context
Describe your training domain, target population, and the behavioral skills or decisions you want to develop.
2
Build the Simulation
Our AI generates a richly populated, behaviorally realistic environment tailored to your scenario — with agents that think and act like real people.
3
Train & Interact
Trainees engage with the simulation, making decisions and navigating dynamic situations. The system adapts in real time to their choices and skill level.
4
Measure & Improve
Detailed analytics reveal performance patterns, blind spots, and growth — guiding the next iteration of training and demonstrating real-world impact.
